Z safe zones · G grid · T add text

Drag on the preview to reframe the video; drag text to move it.
Drag timeline clips onto each other to reorder; drag a clip's edge to trim it.
⌥ Option-drag a clip, voice or music piece to slip it (another part of the source, same place and length).
Drag the round handles on a clip, voice or music piece to fade its sound in / out; double-click a handle for no fade.
Click a voice or music piece, then S cuts it at the playhead and ⌫ deletes it.
